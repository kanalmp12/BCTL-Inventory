# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Students and staff at BUCA Talent checking out equipment (cameras, tools, media production gear) for their projects and daily work.

## Product Purpose
To manage, track, borrow, and return equipment efficiently, preventing stock discrepancies and overdue checkouts.

## Brand Personality
Professional, clean, and reliable (utilizing the deep blue/amber brand colors).

## Anti-references
Cluttered SaaS dashboards with overwhelming metrics, distracting flashy gradients, and excessive nested cards.

## Design Principles
1. Clarity Over Complexity: Visual states must be immediately understandable.
2. Low Cognitive Load: Minimize clicks to borrow and return.
3. Reliability First: Clear confirmation states and error messages.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA compliance with support for high-contrast accessibility modes.
